Route-by-route driving notes

Day 2 · Sun Jun 28

Portland → Saint John → Fundy Rose → Digby

Depart / drive: 7:00 AM · ~7 hrs driving + ferry; ~10 hr day · Signal: Spotty north of Bangor and rural NB

Tonight: Fundy Complex Dockside Suites, Digby (1 room)

Paper directions: Leave Portland early and drive north through Maine toward the US-Canada border. Cross at Calais/St. Stephen with passports ready; budget 10-30 minutes. Continue to Saint John and arrive at the Fundy Rose ferry terminal at least 1 hour before sailing. Take the Fundy Rose from Saint John to Digby; confirm sailing status before leaving Portland. After arrival in Digby, drive directly to the Dockside Suites and keep ferry confirmations printed or saved offline.

Day 3 · Mon Jun 29

Digby → Annapolis Royal → Lunenburg

Depart / drive: 9:00 AM · ~2.5-3 hrs · Signal: Mostly workable, but download maps anyway

Tonight: Smugglers Cove Inn, Lunenburg (1 room)

Paper directions: Leave Digby after breakfast and follow the Annapolis Valley east. Use Annapolis Royal as the primary leg-stretcher if you want a garden or historic stop. Continue toward the South Shore and Lunenburg via the Lighthouse Route where practical. Park once in Lunenburg and walk the UNESCO Old Town and waterfront. Keep the route relaxed; this is the easiest driving day of the loop.

Day 4 · Tue Jun 30

Lunenburg → Halifax/Baddeck corridor → North Sydney ferry

Depart / drive: 8:00 AM · ~5.5 hrs · Signal: Generally available near towns; weaker in rural Cape Breton

Tonight: Marine Atlantic ferry cabin

Paper directions: Leave Lunenburg early enough to protect the Marine Atlantic check-in window. Use Mahone Bay, Chester, Halifax, Truro, or Baddeck only if the ferry timing still has a safe buffer. Arrive at the North Sydney terminal 2 hours before sailing with vehicle documents and reservation details handy. Board the overnight Marine Atlantic ferry to Port aux Basques. Pack an overnight ferry bag before boarding so you do not need the car deck.

Day 5 · Wed Jul 1

Port aux Basques → Corner Brook → Deer Lake → Twillingate

Depart / drive: 7:00 AM · ~7 hrs · Signal: Critical dead zones across rural Newfoundland

Tonight: Anchor Inn Hotel & Suites, Twillingate (1 room)

Paper directions: Disembark at Port aux Basques and begin the Trans-Canada Highway eastbound. Treat Corner Brook as the first serious rest stop and Deer Lake as the natural lunch/fuel stop. Keep fuel above half a tank; services become sparse and cell coverage is unreliable. Turn north toward Twillingate on the Road to the Isles and expect little to no signal. Arrive before dark because moose risk is real, especially at dawn and dusk.

Day 6 · Thu Jul 2

Twillingate → Farewell ferry → Fogo Island Inn

Depart / drive: 8:00 AM · ~2-2.5 hrs if ferry aligns · Signal: Minimal approaching Farewell and very limited on Fogo Island

Tonight: Fogo Island Inn

Paper directions: Leave Twillingate with the Fogo ferry schedule already checked and screenshots saved. Drive toward Farewell with fuel, snacks, and downloaded maps ready. Board the Fogo Island ferry; the crossing is roughly 45 minutes when operating normally. Drive from the island terminal to Joe Batt’s Arm and the Fogo Island Inn. Assume the phone is not a reliable planning tool on Fogo; use this packet and the inn concierge.

Days 7-8 · Fri Jul 3-Sat Jul 4

Fogo Island rest days · no driving

Depart / drive: Stay put · No scheduled drive · Signal: Very limited; rely on inn Wi-Fi and concierge

Tonight: Fogo Island Inn

Paper directions: No major driving is planned on these days. Use the inn concierge for local timing, weather, and any island transfers. Confirm the return ferry plan before the final night on Fogo.

Day 9 · Sun Jul 5

Fogo Island → Farewell → Port aux Basques → North Sydney ferry

Depart / drive: 7:00 AM · Fogo ferry + ~6h15 drive to Port aux Basques · Signal: Very limited until larger Newfoundland towns

Tonight: Marine Atlantic ferry cabin

Paper directions: Leave Fogo early and protect the ferry queue timing back to Farewell. Once back on Newfoundland, drive west across the island toward Port aux Basques. Use Gander, Grand Falls-Windsor, Deer Lake, and Corner Brook as the practical rest/fuel sequence. Avoid arriving at Port aux Basques late; moose risk rises after dusk. Check Marine Atlantic status whenever you have service and keep reservation details printed.

Day 10 · Mon Jul 6

North Sydney → Pictou

Depart / drive: 10:00 AM · ~3 hrs · Signal: Generally available

Tonight: The Scotsman Inn, Pictou (2 rooms)

Paper directions: Disembark in North Sydney and avoid rushing; this is an easier recovery day. Drive west across Nova Scotia toward Pictou. Use Antigonish or New Glasgow as simple coffee/fuel stops if needed. Check in at The Scotsman Inn and keep the afternoon open for the waterfront and Hector Heritage Quay.

Day 11 · Tue Jul 7

Pictou → Moncton/Hopewell Rocks → Fredericton

Depart / drive: 8:00 AM · ~5 hrs driving before long stops · Signal: Mostly available with rural gaps

Tonight: Delta Hotels by Marriott Fredericton (2 rooms)

Paper directions: Leave Pictou and cross from Nova Scotia into New Brunswick via the Amherst corridor. Use Moncton as the practical midpoint for fuel and food. Add Hopewell Rocks only if tide timing and energy make sense; it adds time beyond the roughly five-hour driving day. Otherwise continue direct to Fredericton. Arrive for afternoon check-in at the Delta and plan dinner on Queen Street or near the riverfront.

Day 12 · Wed Jul 8

Fredericton → Maine border → Portland

Depart / drive: 8:00 AM · ~5 hrs plus border time · Signal: Patchy rural NB and northern Maine

Tonight: Home / Portland

Paper directions: Leave Fredericton with passports ready for re-entry into the United States. Cross into Maine and budget time for the border. Use Bangor as the most reliable mid-route stop for fuel, coffee, and food. Consider Freeport only if you want a final easy stop before Portland. Keep offline maps active until you are back in southern Maine.

When the plan changes
