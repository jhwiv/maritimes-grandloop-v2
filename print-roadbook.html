Printer-friendly car packet for poor cell service. Keep with ferry confirmations and offline maps.

Morning launch
  • Fuel above half tank before rural legs.
  • Open today’s map while on Wi-Fi or service.

If cell service drops
  • Do not improvise scenic detours.
  • Continue to the next named town or ferry terminal.
  • Use hotel or ferry staff for status changes.
  • Preserve fixed lodging and ferry nights first.
